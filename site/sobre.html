About

Who makes the game and the questions everyone asks.

Bananie

Bananie is a small studio, created by Jean de Azevedo Pereira, that makes games to play with friends: short matches, lots of laughter and that urge to ask for a rematch. The mascot is Bananinha, Jean's beloved cockatiel, who gives the studio its name and face.

Ninja vs Samurai Mega Blaster Battle Royale was born from missing a classic custom map we used to play for hours.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When does the game launch?

There is no date yet. The game is in development and changes a lot every week. Follow the news.

Which platforms can I play on?

PC (Windows), through Steam.

Steam

How many people can play together?

Up to 16 players per match through Steam. On the same PC, one player on keyboard and mouse and up to 4 controllers can also share the screen. The sweet spot is 5 versus 5, but it works with as few as 2 players.

Can I play alone?

You can practice alone, but the game was made to be played together. The fun starts when the room fills up.

Are there paid items that make characters stronger?

No. Everyone has 1 life and the same attacks. Cosmetics (clothes, hats, weapons) only change your appearance.

Is this the original “Ninja vs Samurai” map?

No. It is a new game inspired by the classic custom map by Shin.hayate and Nintendoman_766. The basic rules are the same (1 life, ninjas stealing talismans, samurai defending), with new modes, maps and controls.

Where can I learn how to play?

In the How to Play guide: controls, classes, abilities, modes, maps and survival tips. You can even print it.
