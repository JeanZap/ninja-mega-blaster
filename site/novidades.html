The development diary: what entered the game, when it entered and why.

11nov 2026

Stone lanterns, ponds and campfires

  • New stone lanterns in the Forest Village and Rival Villages, with lit candles and flickering light.
  • Ponds: crossing water slows you down and shortens your quick dash, just like mud. Think twice before escaping through there.
  • Campfires with cartoon-style flames.
  • More scenery: pine trees, cherry trees, cardboard boxes and mosaic stone paths in the Forest Village.
  • Skins in testing: Adventurer Rat and Ninja Rat for ninjas, and Blue and Red Bird Adventurers for samurai (with katana and bow). They may still change.
10nov 2026

New bushes and more cosmetics

  • Revamped bushes in the Forest Village. They release leaves when someone enters, leaves or moves inside, so stay still if you do not want to be noticed. Leaves do not appear inside the fog of war, so they cannot give anyone away from a distance.
  • New cosmetics: Ninja Scroll on the back, ninja mask, Braided Kasa and six weapons: Katana, Ornate Katana, Ninjatō, Tantō, Shinobi Dagger and Kunai.
  • New cherry trees in the scenery.
9nov 2026

3-hit combo and a sunnier world

  • Sword combo: up to 3 quick slashes in sequence, if each one starts right after the previous one.
  • New animations for sword attacks and guarding.
  • Repainted scenery: grass, flowers, mountains on the horizon and clouds. The maps are now livelier and easier to read.
  • In the main menu, Host Game now comes first, followed by Join Game.
6nov 2026

New map: Cherry Blossom Hills

  • Cherry Blossom Hills replaces Duel Cliffs. It is a huge map for Battle Royale only, with plateaus, ramps, a river, a lake, pagodas and many cherry trees. The high ground is pinkish, making it possible to see from a distance who has the advantage.
  • Timed disguise: the ninja disguise now lasts 3 seconds, with an on-screen countdown.
  • Class weapons: ninjas attack with a dagger and samurai shoot with bow and arrow (visual only; gameplay is the same).
  • Lobby: everyone marks “ready” before starting, you choose the ability when respawning and there is a loading screen.
5nov 2026

Proximity voice chat, elevation and minimap

  • Proximity voice chat (Steam): only people nearby can hear you. You can use “push to talk” (V) or an always-on microphone.
  • Elevation on maps: people on high ground cannot be seen by those below, but attacks still pass through.
  • Minimap that respects the fog of war.
  • New music during matches.
4nov 2026

Flag Capture, classes and cosmetics

  • New Flag Capture mode, with the Rival Villages map.
  • Classes and abilities: one class ability per side and one extra ability chosen each life.
  • Back attack: strikes from behind have greater reach and go through the samurai's guard.
  • Cosmetics: clothing, hats and weapons to customize your character. They never change the game.
  • A Battle Royale zone that closes in, team and color selection, and lobby ping.
3nov 2026

The beginning

  • First game modes: Team Rounds and Battle Royale.
  • Autumn Clearing and Autumn Dense Forest maps.
  • Online matches through Steam.
  • Fog of war, bushes to hide in and sound effects.
