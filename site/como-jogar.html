The golden rule: everyone has 1 life

Inspired by the classic custom map "Ninja vs Samurai", here any hit kills: a slash, a shuriken, a thrust. Nobody wins by trading damage. The winner is the one who positions better, hides, reads the opponent, and attacks at the right moment.

Think before you run. Most of the time, whoever appears first is the one who dies first.

This game is made to be played together

Ninja vs Samurai Mega Blaster is a multiplayer game. You can practice alone, but the real fun begins when the room fills up. The more people join, the funnier and more competitive it gets:

  • More chaos, more laughs: ambushes in bushes, clones fooling everyone, three people dying in the same second, someone disguised as a rock in the middle of the village…
  • More strategy: with full teams, ninjas can split up and attack several talismans at once, while samurai must organize to protect everything.
  • More tension: with 1 life, every additional player is another threat hidden in the fog.
  • More rematches: short matches, and "play again" is just one click away.

Everyone's basic kit

  • Strike: a very fast arcing slash in front of you. It does not pass through walls. Press again right afterward for a combo of up to 3 strikes.
  • Shuriken: 3 charges, recharging one every ~2 s. A hit kills and the shuriken sticks into walls and crates. (Samurai shoot arrows with their bow, which work the same way.)
  • Dash: a short leap during which you cannot be hit. Use it to dodge shurikens and strikes.
Attack from behind! A strike from behind reaches a little farther and even gets through a samurai's guard. Anyone who turns their back to the enemy will not last long.

Dead? It's not over

Depending on the mode, you return after a few seconds with a brief flashing protection. While waiting, the camera follows a living ally. Use the time to change your extra ability: between lives, press the extra ability button to choose the next one.

Ninjas and Samurais

Ninja
Stealthy and clever. Disappears inside bushes and smoke.
Class ability: Disguise, which turns you into the nearest object on the ground (a rock, a crate…) for up to 3 seconds, with an on-screen countdown. Moving or taking any action breaks the disguise.
Samurai
Strong and direct. Cannot hide in bushes, but the strike reaches farther and deflects shurikens.
Class ability: Thrust, which advances 6 m with the spear and kills every enemy in its path.

Game modes

Team Rounds (classic)

Ninjas versus samurai, and nobody respawns during the round. Eliminate the entire enemy team to score a point. The first team to reach the goal (5 points by default) wins.

Ninjas vs Samurais (talismans heist)

The ninjas invade and steal talismans scattered across the map, one at a time, and carry them to the ninja base. The samurai defend.

  • The ninjas share a pool of lives (10 by default). When it runs out, anyone who dies is out.
  • The talisman carrier moves more slowly and glows from time to time, allowing the samurai to find them.
  • Dropped talisman: a samurai who touches it returns it to the shrine; a ninja who touches it picks it up again.
  • Ninjas win by delivering the target number of talismans (5 of 7 by default). Samurai win by depleting the ninjas' lives.

Banner Heist (capture the flag)

Two clans, the Dragon Clan and the Phoenix Clan, each with mixed ninjas and samurai. Steal the enemy banner and take it to your pedestal while yours is safe at home. The carrier moves more slowly and a bell gives them away from time to time. The first to make 3 captures wins, or the team in the lead when 10 minutes run out (a tie goes to sudden death).

Battle Royale

Every player for themselves. Choose ninja or samurai, with no respawns and no shared vision. A zone closes in: staying outside it for 3 seconds means death. The last one standing wins.

A full-room Battle Royale is something else. With 2 people it is a duel; with 10 or more, it is a delightful mess of ambushes, betrayals, and desperate escapes from the zone.

Tips for survival

  • The fog is your friend (and your enemy). You only see what is nearby and what your team sees. Walls and trees block vision.
  • Bushes hide ninjas, but they shake when someone enters, exits, or moves inside. Watch the leaves.
  • Attacking reveals a hidden ninja for a moment. Choose your timing carefully.
  • High ground hides players above from those below. But attacks pass through: you can hit upward.
  • Mud slows you down, including during a dash. Avoid fighting in it.
  • Samurai: deflect shurikens with your strike and send them back to whoever threw them.
  • Ninja: do not fight head-on. Disguise yourself, wait, and attack from behind.
