The Game

Two sides, one hit, no second chance. See the sides, modes and maps.

Choose your side

Ninjas and Samurai

Ninja Stealthy and clever

  • Disappear inside bushes and smoke. The leaves sway when you move, so be careful.
  • Attack with a dagger and throw shurikens.
  • Class ability: Disguise. Become the nearest object (a rock, a box…) for up to 3 seconds.
  • Extras: Smoke Bomb, Clone, Metsubushi (blinding powder), Silent Steps.

Samurai Strong and direct

  • Cannot hide in bushes, but the attack reaches farther and deflects shurikens.
  • Attack from a distance with a bow and arrow.
  • Class ability: Thrust. Dash 6 m with the spear and kill every enemy in the way.
  • Extras: Kiai, Naruko (bell cord), Kamae (guard), Taiko (war drum).

Both sides can also take Quiver, Bamboo Wall or Light Feet. You choose one extra ability each life. Details in the How to Play guide.

Modes

Game modes

Team Rounds classic

Ninjas versus samurai, and nobody respawns during the round. Eliminate the enemy team to score a point. The first to reach the goal (5 by default) wins.

Ninjas vs Samurais

Ninjas steal talismans scattered across the map, one at a time, and take them to the base. Samurai defend. Ninjas win by delivering the goal (5 out of 7 by default); samurai win by using up the ninjas' 10 shared lives.

Battle Royale

Every player for themselves, ninja or samurai, with no respawning or shared vision. The zone closes in: 3 seconds outside it and you are done. With 2 people it is a duel; with 10 or more, it is delicious chaos.

Flag Capture

Two mixed clans, Dragon and Phoenix. The banner carrier moves slowly and a bell gives them away. 3 captures or whoever is ahead at 10 minutes.

Inspiration

Where the idea came from

“Ninja vs Samurai” is a classic custom map, created by Shin.hayate and Nintendoman_766 and played for years in several versions. The idea was simple and brilliant: the ninja tribe must steal talismans from a village protected by samurai, and everyone dies in one hit.

This game keeps what made the original so good (1 life, asymmetric teams, talismans, attacks from behind, limited vision) and adds what we always wanted: Battle Royale, online matches through Steam, proximity voice chat and a colorful, sunny look.
