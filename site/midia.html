In one sentence

Everyone has 1 life: ninjas steal talismans, samurai defend the village, and one strike decides everything.

In one paragraph

Ninja vs Samurai Mega Blaster Battle Royale is a modern take on the classic “Ninja vs Samurai” custom map. Stealthy ninjas hide in bushes and disguise themselves as objects to steal talismans; samurai with greater reach defend the village. No one has a health bar: any hit kills. In addition to team rounds and talisman theft, there is Battle Royale with a closing zone and flag capture, with up to 16 players through Steam.
