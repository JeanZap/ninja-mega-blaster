What it is

Everyone has 1 life

A modern version of the classic custom map “Ninja vs Samurai”. Any hit kills: a slash, a shuriken, a thrust. Nobody wins by trading damage. The winner hides better, reads the opponent and attacks at the right moment.

One hit kills

No health bar, no healing. Every encounter lasts one second and decides everything.

Ninjas versus samurai

Ninjas disappear into bushes and disguise themselves as rocks. Samurai have more range and deflect shurikens.

Made for a crowd

Up to 16 people per match on Steam, with proximity voice chat. The more people, the more chaos.

Fast matches

Short rounds, one-click rematches and cosmetics that never change the game.

Modes

Four ways to die

Team Rounds classic

Ninjas versus samurai, and nobody respawns during the round. Eliminate the entire team to score a point.

Ninjas vs Samurais

Ninjas invade and steal talismans; samurai defend the village. Ninjas share a pool of lives: when it is gone, it is over.

Battle Royale

Every player for themselves, no respawning, with a zone that closes in. The last one standing wins.

Flag Capture

Dragon Clan versus Phoenix Clan. Steal the enemy banner and bring it home while yours is safe.
